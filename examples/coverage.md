## Carry-over rules for later families

Read this before curating any family after aggregators.

- **Sweep depth (owner, 2026-10-01): proper sweep by default.** Every category gets an exhaustive sweep, not a quick pass: owner-named places, then systematic venue lists (city "uit" agenda venue directories, Partyflock/RA location lists, platform city pages, organisers' venue lists, sector directories), then open searches per city. When in doubt, include: the owner prefers removing entries later over missing them. Use a quick sweep only if the owner asks for it.
- **Hijacked domains are always excluded (owner, 2026-10-01):** check each page's title; a domain now serving gambling, casino, parking, or for-sale content is never recorded, and a link pointing to one is called out in `notes`.
- **Address conflicts: own site wins (owner, 2026-10-04).** When sources disagree on an address or postcode, the venue's or organiser's own site (contact page, footer, route page) beats directories, ticketing sites, agendas, and OpenStreetMap; the note records the resolution and the losing value. If the own site is itself inconsistent, it stays an open item.
- **Hidden prices are left (owner, 2026-10-04):** a bookable whose price only appears inside a booking widget keeps "price in the booking flow" as its note; nobody steps through booking flows to fetch prices. The Bookings view links to the booking page.
- **Missing coordinates are acceptable (owner, 2026-10-01):** if Nominatim has no house-level or named-place match, record the address in `notes` as an open item and omit `venue`. Never estimate.
- **Browser-only sources are fine (owner, 2026-10-01):** set `reading_hint: browser`; the scanner uses its headless browser for them.

- **Kids-only is out (owner, 2026-10-03).** Don't record sources whose public programme is aimed only at children or at families with children (youth and puppet theatres, children's museums, children's film festivals, family listing sites), nor kids-only bookables (children's parties, kids' workshops, school programmes). Mixed venues stay. Applied retroactively on 2026-10-03: 11 approved entries removed (see Rejected sources).
- **Adult-only is in (owner, 2026-10-03), in every category.** Don't skip or reject a source for being 18+ (kink/fetish and sex-positive parties, swingers-club party nights, burlesque and erotic shows, erotic museums and art, shibari/tantra/sex-ed workshops, cannabis culture). Note "18+" in `notes`. Paid sexual services are not events or bookables. The 18+ backfill of the approved families is done (2026-10-03); from now on 18+ is a normal type: include it when it comes up, no separate checks or searches (owner, 2026-10-03).
- **Culture-other decisions (owner, 2026-10-03):** merged all 201 finds; guided walks stay one bookable per route (Gilde Rotterdam 43, Gilde Den Haag 32, Architour Rotterdam 9, smaller Gilde sets elsewhere); Het Utrechts Archief and Molen de Ster moved to museums (consistent with Stadsarchief Amsterdam and Molen van Sloten); art institutions (Melly, Brutus, RADIUS), WORM, The Grey Space, Conny Janssen, Dansateliers, Cultuurhuis Delft, university-run spaces (VU ART SCIENCE, AG/HKU, Parnassos, CREA), Dome Concerts and LovingRoom stay culture-other. - **Bookables are a priority (owner, 2026-10-03):** check every source for group/private tours, workshops on request, and tastings, with price and group size; don't leave "details not checked".

- **Rate limits:** DJGuide blocks an IP after about a dozen quick requests. Space out checks on any single domain.
- **Newsletters (scope added 2026-10-01, taxonomy §6):** for every source, check whether it announces events by email. If it does, add a `newsletter` link (the signup page) and `newsletter_subscribed: false`. If there's a public archive (Mailchimp campaign-archive, Substack, Laposta), add it as a separate `website` link. Record it even when the source also has an agenda page.
- **Bookables (scope added 2026-10-01, taxonomy §8):** for every source, record activities offered on request or by appointment (private tastings, workshops, tours, karaoke rooms). Table reservations, ticket sales, and corporate venue hire don't count. A source with no public agenda but a newsletter or a bookable is no longer automatically rejected.
- **Late finds are always backfilled, without asking (owner, 2026-10-01):** a source found while curating a later family is added to its already-approved family. Verify it like any other entry, note "late find, backfilled" with the date in `notes`, update that family's coverage cells, and mention it at the next checkpoint.
